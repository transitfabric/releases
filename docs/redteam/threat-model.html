Threat model

Assume every miner is rational and adversarial, some validators are lazy or colluding, and the subnet owner is trusted only as far as the published code.

T19 HEADER PART SETTLED (ADR-0037, proposed); other leaks stand. Overlay links carry plain inner IPv6 with no class on the packet, so a relay has no class byte to read, mask, flip or forge, and hop receipts carry one unsplit total, never per-class counters. A relay that serves canaries well and degrades paid traffic can no longer tell them apart by a header byte or be paid differently per class on its hop (sim class_relabeller). Still OPEN: source address (public probe keys), size/rate profile (datapath.md §Residual leaks), and canary credit, which now has to come from the validators' own records (OQ-17). Canary plans (Task 14, ADR-0051, ADR-0053; not scored until PB-38). Canary-stream timing is no longer public: each validator's timing, sizes and classes come from its own secret plan, committed in its manifest at window open and revealed after the window ends (measurement.md §Canary plans). Which pairs carry how many bytes is a public draw from the committed seed and snapshot, weighted by bounded deduped verified capacity and capped per node, per UID and per path, and every validator's reveal must spend exactly its stake share of every pair. Shaping follows fabric-wide GlobalMarginals only (the default mix until PB-38 or PB-39 adds a producer). Validator-run nodes source and sink the stream and hold its sampling keys. Canary bytes come out of receipt usage and never reach a paid-traffic trigger. What is hidden: within an allocated pair a node can't predict a flow's start, size or class, and guesses on them gain nothing beyond sampling noise (sims canary_favourer, canary_slot_presence). What isn't: the pair (T145), and the packet shape and validator source address (T148). validator_canary_alloc_favour fails its reveal; canary_alloc_node_count_sybil gains nothing per unit of deduped verified capacity, and canary_alloc_uid_split holds one UID to the water-filled cap. Hop crediting order (ADR-0037 fix rounds 1-3). Unsplit totals made the greedy crediting pass order-dependent. Fix round 3 replaced it with order-free crediting (scoring.md §Byte crediting 0).
  • One-hop griefing (rounds 1-2). Uncovered traffic on a 1-hop route no longer marks a shared honest hop inflated, with or without an allowlist (one_hop_free_griefer).
  • F1 (HIGH). A covered 1-hop route could swallow a shared hop and get it flagged, zeroing the paid paths through it (covered_one_hop_swallow).
  • F2 (HIGH). A 1-hop route's inflated weight could carry a regret-gated path's bytes on the shared hop into an ungated path (one_hop_weight_regret_bypass, also with a shared dedupe group).
  • F3 (MEDIUM). A covered path could take another path's share of a shared hop and get that path's next hop flagged (validator test adr0037_fix3_a_covered_path_never_takes_another_paths_share).

What the sims and tests prove:

  • Honest traffic never flags a hop (by construction, and by the 500-seed property test, allowlist on and off).
  • A forged 1-byte client receipt never lowers any hop's weighted credit. Per dedupe entity, it moves at most [SCR-10]'s floor remainder, one byte of each class per incident hop, with random regret gates, dedupe groups and pairs with two paths (fix round 4).
  • A gated or suppressed path that carried nothing never lowers anyone's credit (fix round 4, T23 Finding 1, borrowed_weight_gate_griefer and the property test).
  • F1 costs its victims nothing in either mode.
  • F2 gains nothing with the allowlist. With a bought voucher of Q bytes it captures at most Q * (1 + tol) per shared hop (fix round 4, LOW). Without the allowlist, the 1-hop route's free is credited (T16).
  • An inflated hop under the allowlist earns at most its attested free.
  • Colluding adjacent hops hiding inflation behind a 1-hop route earn no more than fabricating on that route alone (adjacent_hops_hide_inflation).

Not proved: inflation is now flagged only above the sum of path bottlenecks, which is looser than the greedy pass when paths overlap.
