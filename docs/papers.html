Two papers describe Transit Fabric. Both are in English and Polish.

One measured network from the edge to the data center

A short paper: what the network is for, who it serves, and why its incentives hold together. No formulas.

  • Polski (PDF)

Whitepaper, version 1.4 (2026-11-20; names updated 2026-11-25)

The technical design: identity and addressing, the data path, measurement, scoring for both mechanisms, verifying weights, and the economics. It also says what is live on testnet and what comes later.

  • English (PDF)
  • Polski (PDF)

Each paper describes the project as of its date. What is live on testnet today is on the Known limits page. The red-team pack no longer carries a copy of the whitepaper; these PDFs are the current ones.

Neither paper is an offer or a promise of returns. Where a paper and the specifications disagree, the specifications win.
